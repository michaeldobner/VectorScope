# User guide

[Deutsche Version](../de/bedienung.md) · [Overview](README.md)

## Views

| Width | Layout |
|---|---|
| iPhone, iPad in Split View (below 760 points) | One column. At the bottom the bar with **2 min**, the sections to swipe and the slider for the settings. A story opens as a sheet from the bottom |
| iPad portrait (760 to 1099 points) | Two columns: stories on the left, the open story on the right. The bar stays at the bottom |
| iPad landscape (from 1100 points) | Three columns: sections, briefing and settings on the left, stories in the middle, the open story on the right |

**Look at the top, operate at the bottom.** Below the status bar and the Dynamic Island there is a solid strip with nothing to tap. Newer iOS versions draw this area blurred, icons there look washed out. At the very top there is only a quiet line with the time of the edition and the number of sources and countries.

## Sections

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Top** | The lead story with a large picture, places 2 and 3, the other view as a strip to swipe, places 4 to 6 |
| **Other view** | German-speaking: stories without a DACH source but with at least two international ones. Global: stories without an international source but with at least two from DACH |
| **DE · CH · AT** | Germany, Switzerland, Austria. In German it is called "DACH" |
| **World, Business, Tech, Science, Culture, Sport** | Up to 30 stories each, new first, then by importance |

Swiping left or right on the list turns to the next section. A blue dot on a section means there is something new. Every list ends with "You're up to date" and a button to the next section, the list is not endless.

## A story

Above the headline every card shows how many sources from how many countries report, and how old the newest report is. The lead story also shows the country codes.

A tap opens the story, on the iPhone as a sheet at half height. Pull up for everything, swipe down to close, or tap **Close** or next to the sheet. The story shows:

* **Headline and teaser** of the most factual outlet, below it where the text comes from: "From Tagesschau", "Translated from BBC" or "In the words of SRF".
* **How they report it:** the countries and the **perspectives**, one card per outlet with country, time and its own headline. A tap opens the article at the outlet. Translated titles are marked "translated", the original is shown small below.
* **All sources** by group: public broadcasters, quality press, international, business, tech media, fast news.

**Read original** at the bottom of the sheet opens the article of the outlet whose text is shown. In the app on the Home Screen, iOS opens articles in a window above the app, **Done** leads back.

## 2-minute briefing

**2 min** at the bottom left shows the ten most important stories, one per screen, with picture, headline and teaser: the six of the Top page plus the first of DE · CH · AT, World, Business and Tech. On the iPad in landscape picture and text stand side by side.

| Gesture or button | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Next ›**, tap on the right, swipe left | Next story |
| **‹ Back**, tap in the left third, swipe right | Previous story |
| Touch and hold | Pause, releasing goes on |
| Swipe down, **Stop** | Close the briefing |
| **Share** | Pass on the original article, see [Sharing](#sharing) |
| **More on this** | Open the story with every perspective |

After 11 seconds the next story comes by itself, the bar at the bottom shows the time, "3 / 10" the place. On the first opening a hint explains the gestures, never again after that. At the end: "You're up to date". Progress and buttons sit at the bottom, not at the camera.

## Pictures

A picture is never cut. It stands whole in a 16:9 frame, the free edge is filled with the same picture enlarged and blurred. A picture smaller than its frame keeps its size instead of being blown up and blurred.

## Sharing

**Share** in the briefing and in every story opens the share sheet of the device, for Messages, WhatsApp or Mail, with the headline and the link to the original article of the outlet whose text is shown. Without a share sheet the link is copied. A link to the story in NEWS itself does not exist yet.

## What is new

* **Blue dot:** the story appeared after your last visit and is not opened yet. On the very first visit everything of the last two hours counts as new.
* **"+3 sources":** three outlets joined since your last visit.
* **"You read up to here":** separates new from known in the sections, with the time of your last visit.
* **"↑ 4 new stories":** the app asks for a new edition every 5 minutes. When it brings new stories, the content does not jump, this notice appears at the bottom. A tap brings them in.

A visit ends when you leave the app or send it to the background.

## Settings

The slider icon at the bottom right, on the iPad in landscape **View and language**:

| Setting | Choice | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| App language | Deutsch, English | Controls, sections, notices, dates |
| Reports | Deutsch, English, Original | Headlines, teasers and titles of the sources. Original shows every text as its source wrote it, also French from RTS and Italian from RSI |
| Perspective | German-speaking, Global | What is important and what the other view shows, see [Stories and importance](stories.md) |
| View | Calm, Pictures | Calm: small preview pictures. Pictures: every story with a picture shown large |

On the first start app language, reports and perspective follow the language of the device: German and German-speaking on a German device, else English and global. Times are always shown in the time zone of the device. So a friend abroad can use NEWS without setting anything.

## Offline

NEWS keeps the last edition loaded on the device and shows it with the notice "No connection". When an edition is older than 45 minutes, its age is shown above the list. Without any edition so far, **Try again** appears.

## Install

Open the address of the own server with `/news/` in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. NEWS then starts as its own app, without the address bar.
